--- PAGE 1 ---
Hot cooked food intended for immediate distribution must be held at or above 60 degrees
Celsius
from the point of preparation until handover to the recipient. If the food falls below this
temperature for more than two hours, it must be discarded and not redistributed.

--- PAGE 2 ---
Raw meat and poultry must never be transported in the same insulated container as cooked
or
ready-to-eat food, even if both are sealed separately. Use a dedicated container for raw
items,
clearly labelled, and clean it with food-safe sanitiser after every run.

--- PAGE 3 ---
Frozen items must remain at or below minus 18 degrees Celsius throughout transport. A
volunteer
transporting both frozen and hot items in the same vehicle must use two separate insulated
containers, never a shared compartment.

--- PAGE 4 ---
All perishable donations must be labelled with the original expiry or best-before date from the
donor. Items within six hours of their stated expiry may only be distributed to recipients
capable
of immediate consumption, such as shelters with on-site dining.

--- PAGE 5 ---
Volunteers handling food donations must complete the NGO's basic food hygiene briefing
before their
first delivery. This covers handwashing, container sanitation, and recognising visible spoilage
such
as discolouration or off odours.

--- PAGE 6 ---
Allergen information supplied by the donor, such as the presence of peanuts, shellfish, or
gluten,
must be written on the outside of the container in English, so recipient staff can see it without
opening the packaging.

--- PAGE 7 ---
A delivery vehicle used for food transport must not have carried chemicals, cleaning agents,
or
non-food cargo in the same compartment within the preceding 24 hours, unless the
compartment has
been fully cleaned and aired out.
